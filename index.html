About

I’m an undergraduate at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, studying Computer Science and Mathematics.

I’m interested in Numerical Optimization and Mathematical Optimization. My research focuses on low-rank optimization and large-scale numerical methods, with an emphasis on designing efficient algorithms and understanding the geometry of optimization problems.

My recent work explores preconditioning and first-order methods for semidefinite optimization.

I’m fortunate to be supervised by Richard Y. Zhang during my undergraduate studies.

Research interests

  • Numerical optimization
  • Numerical linear algebra
  • Preconditioning
  • Randomized algorithms
  • Low-rank methods
  • Large-scale computation
02

Research

Optimization & controlOngoing work

Large-scale semidefinite optimization

I study algorithms for finding common Lyapunov certificates, with a focus on initialization, first-order methods, and adaptive preconditioning. The goal is to reach a valid stability certificate efficiently.

How can an algorithm use the geometry of the certificate it is trying to find?

Supervised by Richard Y. Zhang.

In collaboration with Madeleine Udell.

Randomized numerical linear algebra

Parallel randomized pivoted Cholesky

I explore randomized pivot selection for low-rank matrix approximation, including error analysis and the trade-offs between communication and computation in parallel algorithms.

Supervised by Edgar Solomonik.
In collaboration with Zhanrui Zhang.

Nonconvex optimization

The geometry of low-rank factorization

My work on nonnegative matrix factorization examines theoretical guarantees, the structure of stationary points, and descent and negative-curvature directions.

Supervised by Richard Y. Zhang.
