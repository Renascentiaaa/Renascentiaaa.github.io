About

I’m an undergraduate at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, studying Computer Science and Mathematics.

I’m interested in Numerical Optimization and Mathematical Optimization. My research focuses on low-rank optimization and large-scale numerical methods, with an emphasis on designing efficient algorithms and understanding the geometry of optimization problems.

My recent work explores preconditioning and first-order methods for semidefinite optimization.

I’m fortunate to be supervised by Richard Y. Zhang during my undergraduate studies.

Research interests

  • Numerical optimization
  • Numerical linear algebra
  • Preconditioning
  • Randomized algorithms
  • Low-rank methods
  • Large-scale computation

Preconditioned Low-Rank Simultaneous Lyapunov Stability Problem

Designing algorithm for finding common Lyapunov certificates, with a focus on initialization, first-order methods, and adaptive preconditioning. The goal is to reach a stability certificate efficiently.

How can an algorithm use the geometry of the certificate it is trying to find?

Supervised by Richard Y. Zhang.
In collaboration with Madeleine Udell.
